MIT was selected as the default for this local source candidate on 2026-09-12 under the open-source release request. Preserve the license and copyright notice in redistributed copies.

OBS and BlackHole are separately installed products. Their source and binaries are not bundled in this source preview, and this project's license does not relicense them. Before creating a combined installer, review their applicable distribution requirements, including [BlackHole’s developer licensing guidance](https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole#developer-guides). Bundling clearance is still a release gate.

Contributors must identify copied material and establish its provenance and permission. A software license does not grant rights to music played through the app.
